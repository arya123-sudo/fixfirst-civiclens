The Problem — Cities Don't Lack Complaints. They Lack Clarity.

Municipal authorities receive thousands of complaints through apps, WhatsApp and social media. The failure is not collection — it is understanding.

Scattered Reports

Complaints arrive through multiple channels with no unified view.

Unstructured Data

Reports are vague and submitted in different languages and scripts.

Incorrect Routing

Complaints reach the wrong departments and bounce between desks.

Duplicate Reports

Multiple citizens report the same incident as separate tickets.

Poor Prioritization

Critical issues get buried under routine complaints, first-come-first-served.

Vague Reports

“Road bad” carries no actionable detail — most systems guess anyway.

Core problem: authorities see individual complaints instead of understanding the actual civic incidents behind them.

The Solution — From Citizen Reports to Actionable Incidents

The same workflow you can run live in the demo above:

STEP 1

Citizen Input

English, ಕನ್ನಡ or हिन्दी complaint, with optional photo and what is nearby.

STEP 2

AI Understanding

Language identification, classification into 6 categories, severity with a one-line reason, department routing (BBMP · BWSSB · BESCOM).

STEP 3

Photo Evidence Check

The moment a photo is attached, an on-device check analyses its dimensions, lighting and sharpness, flags low-light or blurry evidence for the officer, and adds +10 credibility. Honest scope: detecting the hazard inside the image is the production Vision API step.

STEP 4

Incident Fusion

Complaints describing the same real-world problem fuse into one Incident with supporting reports.

STEP 5

Contextual Risk Boost

Surroundings — a school, a hospital — re-weight severity and priority.

STEP 6

Municipal Dashboard

Unified incidents ranked by explainable priority, with SLA countdowns and auto-escalation to the Ward Engineer.

Output: structured, explainable and prioritised civic incidents — not a pile of tickets.

Innovation 1 — Incident Fusion

One real-world problem. Multiple reports. One unified Incident. Every new complaint is scored against open Incidents on four signals:

SignalWeightWhat it evaluates
Location30Same ward / area
Category15Same type of civic issue
Semantic Similarity40Meaning overlap of the complaint text
Time Window15Reported within the active incident window
75+  Auto-merge as a supporting report 50–74  Officer Review Queue — a human decides <50  New Incident

Life-safety rule: a Critical hazard always raises its own Incident immediately — fusion never delays it. Live proof on this page: the duplicate pothole fuses at 84/100 and its priority rises to 93; the Kannada garbage report scores 60/100 across languages and waits for a human instead of being silently merged.

Innovation 2 — Contextual Risk Boost

The same issue. Different surroundings. Different levels of risk. CivicLens evaluates what is near the incident:

School / College — exposure of children Hospital / Clinic — patients & emergency access Market / Bus Stop — public exposure Highway / Main Road — traffic & accident risk Lake / Water Source — environmental impact
Explainable priority, worked example (run it above): a live wire near a school scores Severity 55 + Zone 20 + Urgency 15 = 90/100 — and reaches 100 with a photo attached.
Full formula: Priority = Severity + Zone + Community + Photo + Urgency — every point is shown on the ticket, so an officer can defend the order to citizens and councillors.

Technology & Implementation

Built for practical civic intelligence — and for a demo that cannot fail on stage:
